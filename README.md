# FixIt – Intelligent Household Troubleshooting Assistant

**Module:** Intelligent Interactive Systems  
**Project Type:** Interactive Figma Prototype  
**Status:** Prototype developed; diagnostic behaviour simulated.

## 1. Project Overview

FixIt is a mobile interface prototype designed to help students, tenants and other non-expert users understand common household problems.

It provides guided troubleshooting through three scenarios:

- **Water leakage:** Identifying the location and severity of a leak.
- **Electrical sparking:** Recognising potential hazards and directing users towards appropriate assistance.
- **Washing machine drainage:** Using symptom-based questions to identify possible drainage issues.

The aim is to make household troubleshooting easier to understand through a simple, guided interaction.

## 4. How to Explore

1. Open the Figma link above.
2. Enter Present mode.
3. Start troubleshooting from the home screen.
4. Select one of the available household problem categories.
5. Answer the guided questions.
6. Follow the displayed result and recommended next steps.

## 5. Development Record

The project was developed through the following stages:

1. Defined the household troubleshooting problem and target users.
2. Identified three scenarios: water leakage, electrical sparking and washing-machine drainage.
3. Designed the mobile interface and navigation in Figma.
4. Created guided questions and corresponding outcome screens.
5. Added safety warnings and further-assistance pathways.
6. Connected the screens using Figma prototype interactions.
7. Reviewed the prototype pathways and refined the design.

## 6. Testing

The prototype was reviewed through interaction walkthroughs of the three troubleshooting scenarios.

Testing focused on:
- Navigation between screens.
- Selection of diagnostic answers.
- Display of the intended outcome screens.
- Visibility of electrical safety warnings.
- Access to further assistance and resolution options.

This was a prototype walkthrough, not a formal user study or validation of real household diagnoses.

## 7. AI Assistance

AI assistance was used in a limited supporting role for brainstorming, organising project ideas and improving written documentation. The project design, Figma screens and prototype connections were assembled by the me. Suggestions were reviewed and adapted during development.

## 8. Limitations and Future Work

The current prototype uses predefined interaction pathways and cannot independently diagnose household faults. Future development could include a validated diagnostic knowledge base, a real AI-supported system, persistent troubleshooting history and professional service integration.
